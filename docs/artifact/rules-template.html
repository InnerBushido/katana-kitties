How a rule gets made, and how an unwanted one gets in
Route 1 · intended

You say it

A session writes your words into CLAUDE.md. docs.md then says to copy it into PROJECT.md §9 and the Field Guide §9. That makes three hand-kept copies.

Route 2

A bug becomes a rule

A playtest breaks something, the fix gets a world-check assertion, and the reasoning goes into a design note. The wording is often Claude's, not yours.

Route 3 · invisible

Claude's memory

When you correct Claude, it saves a memory outside the repo. It loads every session, and one of them says it overrides CLAUDE.md.

Route 4 · drift

Prose escalation

A note calls something "the Nth non-negotiable, read the other way round", and later sessions treat that reading as law.

What would stop it. Make RULES.md the only place a rule is written. Generate the CLAUDE.md block and both §9 sections from it, the way §5 and §6 already generate from code. Have world-check fail when a copy drifts. Add one standing instruction: Claude may propose a rule, but never adds one without your OK, and every rule records who decided it and when.
