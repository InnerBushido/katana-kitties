剣 Sprite editor

    Subject (A)

    forward / up are in her heights; forward follows the mirror. The code's sizes stay underneath: 1 is what the game drew before this file existed.

    Reference (B)

    View

    Mouse: wheel zooms, right- or middle-drag pans, Shift+right-drag or Shift+wheel tilts. Left-drag moves A.

    Play

    Every group has a Stand vs … sequence: B stands in her default pose while A plays the group.

    Keys

    1–9 pose in group (A)
    Space play / pause
    , . step
    [ ] slower / faster
    ←→ forward
    ↑↓ up
    - = scale
    Shift finer
    F flip facing
    Tab swap A/B
    O B opacity
    A D B apart
    Ctrl+Z undo
    Ctrl+Y redo
    Ctrl+S save
    R reset view
    IJKL pan
    T G tilt

    Sheet

    Tiles: click to show it; Shift-click drops it or adds it back; Alt-click mirrors it. A tile says img N and which slot it plays in.
    Untick a design to stop the game offering it. Homes that already chose it keep their choice and draw the first design that is on, until it is ticked again — nothing is lost.
