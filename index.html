HOW TO PLAY

ON FOOT

  • Move — stick / WASD
  • Jump — A / Space (twice to double jump)
  • Slash — X / F — knock everything over! (a claw swipe when you're on a panda)
  • Sprint — ZL/ZR / Shift
  • Ride dragon or panda — Y / Q when the ring glows
  • Join a clan — B / E standing in a shrine ring
  • Map zoom — Z (and X for Frost when split)

ON A DRAGON

  • Fly — stick
  • Up — A / Space
  • Down — B / E
  • Boost — ZL/ZR / Shift
  • Breathe — X / F — every dragon breathes something different
  • Dive low and fast to scatter a whole market
  • Hop off anywhere — your dragon flies home by itself

Bamboo can only be cut with a katana — no dragon can burn it. Land, get off, and slash your way through the grove east of town. Every cane is worth double.

Six clans, six islands, six powers. Look for a coloured beam of light — that's a shrine. Stand in its ring and press interact to join. Thunderpaw run faster, Riverclaw have a longer katana, Shadowtail can triple jump, Windwhisker dragons breathe twice as far, and Icewhisker can sense mischief — an arrow points at the nearest thing you haven't knocked over yet, which is how you find the last few. You can swap any time by finding another shrine, and you two don't have to pick the same one.

Pandapaw is different — you have to earn it. Its shrine is on the bamboo island. Swear the oath, then go and cut 20 bamboo and a panda cub will come and follow you around. Cut 20 more and it grows big enough to ride — twice as fast as you can run, jumps higher, and slash becomes a huge claw swipe that cuts about four bamboo at once. Your badge counts down the canes for you.

You each raise your own — Ember's is Bao, Frost's is Mochi — and it stays yours even if you join a different clan later. But a grown panda only follows a Pandapaw kitten: join somewhere else and it waits where you left it, like a dragon on its perch, and shows up on the map so you can go back for it.

Player 2 uses the arrow keys and the numpad, or plug in a second controller. The screen splits automatically when you run apart and joins back up when you're together. The map in the corner shows both of you, every dragon, and everywhere worth going.
